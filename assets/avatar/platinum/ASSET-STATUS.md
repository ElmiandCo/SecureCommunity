Platinum avatar integration checkpoint

The Platinum directory is intentionally separate from the composited/base avatar layers.

Required final files:
- male-platinum.png — approved silver/Platinum male avatar
- female-platinum.png — approved silver/Platinum female avatar

Integration rule:
- Selecting Platinum replaces the displayed avatar image.
- Do not render the base avatar behind Platinum.
- Gender selection controls which Platinum asset is available.
- Platinum save/access is gated at 10,000 XP.
