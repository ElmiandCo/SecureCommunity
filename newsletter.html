The Community Table

Should Our Mosques Make More Room for Youth Voices?

A lively community debate is not necessarily a disagreement to be won. It can be a chance to listen carefully, ask better questions and discover what younger members need from the spaces they inherit.

This sample feature explores a simple idea: youth councils, open forums and intergenerational conversations can give more people a meaningful seat at the table while keeping respect at the center.

Building Community

A New Mosque Begins With a Foundation of People

Before the first wall rises, a mosque project usually begins with conversations: What will the community need? How will children learn? Where can families gather? How can the building serve neighbors as well as worshippers?

This sample story follows the human side of mosque construction — volunteers, planners, donors, architects and families imagining a place that can grow with the community.

The Nikah Table: Small Details That Become Big Memories

From the seating plan to the meal, wedding days are full of small decisions. The best celebrations often leave room for the people who matter most — grandparents, children, friends, neighbors and the couple themselves.

A simple program, clear communication and a little flexibility can turn a busy day into a gathering people remember with gratitude.
